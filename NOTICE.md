## M5GFX / LovyanGFX — MIT

`simulator/native/include/font_metrics.hpp` is generated by
`scripts/generate-font-metrics.py` from the M5GFX package that PlatformIO
installs for the firmware builds. It reproduces **measurements only** — the
per-glyph advance and bearing numbers of `Font2`, `FreeSansBold18pt7b`, and
`FreeSansBold24pt7b`. No glyph bitmap is copied into this repository.

`simulator/native/include/sim_text.hpp` reimplements the geometry of
`LGFXBase::text_width` and `LGFXBase::draw_string` so the simulator measures
text the way the panel does.

- M5GFX: https://github.com/m5stack/M5GFX (MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 M5Stack)
- LovyanGFX: https://github.com/lovyan03/LovyanGFX (FreeBSD/BSD-2-Clause)

The `FreeSans*` faces that M5GFX ships originate in Adafruit-GFX, which derives
them from GNU FreeFont. Only their metrics are measured here.

## M5Unified, M5Stack examples

Firmware under `firmware/` targets M5Unified and follows the M5Stack API. No
M5Stack source is vendored; PlatformIO resolves those packages at build time
from `platformio.ini`.
